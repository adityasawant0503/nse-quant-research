Volatility edge

Option prices in India usually assume Nifty will move more than it actually does. India VIX, the market's own forecast of the next month's volatility, has been higher than what followed on most days since 2008. Option sellers collect that gap. This study measures it and tests three ways of selling Nifty options, with every NSE charge.
