What is this?

Two kinds of things live here. Tools answer questions you have before a trade: how many shares to buy, what an option strategy can make or lose. Research tests famous trading ideas on 20+ years of real Nifty data, with real NSE charges, to see which ones actually work.

Every number is calculated live in your browser. Nothing you type leaves this page.

Hedging lab

Each month since 2001, sell a one-month at-the-money Nifty option and delta-hedge it with Nifty futures along the price path that really happened. There are no historical option prices here, so implied volatility is trailing 21-day realised volatility plus the markup you choose.

Value at Risk

Every month from 2002 to 2023, a Monte Carlo model fitted on the previous year forecasts next month's worst-case Nifty loss. The bars show what actually happened. A 99% VaR should be broken about once every 100 months.

Breaches by model

Fat tails (Student-t) barely help over a month, because 21 daily shocks average out. Weighting recent days (EWMA) makes it worse: markets are often calm just before they crash.

Pairs trading

69 same-sector Nifty 50 pairs, tested for cointegration on Oct 2020 – Sep 2023 and then traded from Oct 2023 to Sep 2025 with the hedge ratio frozen. Both legs are costed as stock futures, because a cash-market short can't be held overnight in India.
